# Yare Construction — plan

## What this is
This is not a paying client — it's a fictional demo build for Creek's own marketing. Adam is running Facebook outreach and paid ads promoting free website mock-ups, and wants a real, deployed "after" site to (1) use as before/after reveal footage in a Reel and (2) add to Creek's portfolio permanently, since Creek currently has zero examples in the trades category despite trades (builders, plumbers, electricians, roofers) being the strongest-performing category in Adam's outreach. Treat it exactly like a real client build in terms of quality — it needs to look and feel like a genuine small building firm's website, not a showcase piece — but there is no real "Yare Construction" business behind it. Name checked (2026-08-30): no existing UK builder trading as "Yare Construction" found.

## Business & industry
A small general building firm based in Norwich, Norfolk — extensions, renovations, loft conversions, and general building work for homeowners. A small team (owner-operator plus a couple of tradespeople), not a large firm.

## Primary goal
Quote/enquiry generation — a homeowner should be able to look at the site and want to submit an enquiry for a project.

## Audience
Homeowners researching builders for a specific project (extension, loft conversion, renovation). Non-technical, comparing a handful of local builders, and — per builders specifically — care a lot about seeing evidence of past work (a strong portfolio/gallery matters more here than for most trades) and trust signals (insurance, experience, reviews), since the spend is high-ticket and there's real risk anxiety about picking the wrong builder.

## Brand assets
None exist — this is a new fictional business, no existing logo/colours/fonts to carry over.
