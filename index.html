WHO WE ARE

Built the way we'd build for ourselves.

Yare Construction is a small design-and-build team working across Norfolk and Suffolk. We keep projects small enough that the people who draw them are the people who build them — no call centres, no handoffs between departments that have never met. Every job gets the same level of attention, whether it's a single-storey extension or a ground-up build on the river.
